Pilot Operations

Daily Admin Checklist

  • Check new paid and confirmed trips.
  • Assign available drivers or couriers to waiting trips.
  • Monitor active trips until completion.
  • Confirm completed trips credit the driver wallet.
  • Review pending payout requests.
  • Mark payout as paid only after money has been sent to the driver.

Driver Onboarding Rules

  • Confirm the driver or courier name and phone number before approval.
  • Confirm driver type: car driver or bike courier.
  • Confirm vehicle or bike availability before assigning real trips.
  • Confirm the driver's Abuja service area and availability window.
  • Approve only drivers and couriers who are ready for pilot testing.
  • Tell approved drivers to sign in with their phone number and secure login code.
  • Run one test assignment with each new driver before sending real rider trips.

Trip Assignment Rules

  • Only assign drivers to trips with payment status PAID or Pay on Dropoff trips.
  • For prepaid trips, make sure commitment is CONFIRMED before assignment.
  • Assign one driver or courier at a time.
  • Do not reassign completed or cancelled trips.

Payment & Payout Rules

  • Prepaid trips must show PAID before dispatch.
  • Driver earnings are credited after trip completion.
  • Payout requests should be checked against the driver wallet balance.
  • After sending bank transfer to the driver, mark the payout as paid in Admin Payouts.

Support Notes

  • If a rider payment succeeds but status is not updated, check Trip Status again first.
  • If a driver cannot see an assigned trip, confirm the driver phone number is correct.
  • If a trip is cancelled, do not assign a driver.
  • Keep rider and driver communication clear and simple during the pilot.
