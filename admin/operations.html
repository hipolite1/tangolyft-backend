Operations

Before Launch Checklist

  • Confirm DEBUG_OTP is set to false in Render.
  • Confirm app.tangomove.com loads Rider, Driver, and Admin pages.
  • Confirm Paystack callback returns riders to app.tangomove.com/rider/status.html.
  • Confirm Paystack webhook is active and payments show PAID / CONFIRMED.
  • Complete one rider payment, admin assignment, driver completion, and wallet credit test.
  • Complete one driver payout request and admin mark-paid test.
  • Confirm support contact and operations operator are ready before real rider use.

Daily Admin Checklist

  • Check new paid and confirmed trips.
  • Assign available drivers or couriers to waiting trips.
  • Monitor active trips until completion.
  • Confirm completed trips credit the driver wallet.
  • Review pending payout requests.
  • Mark payout as paid only after money has been sent to the driver.

Driver Onboarding Rules

  • Confirm the driver or courier name and phone number before approval.
  • Confirm driver type: car driver or bike courier.
  • Confirm vehicle or bike availability before assigning real trips.
  • Confirm the driver's Abuja service area and availability window.
  • Approve only drivers and couriers who are ready for launch testing.
  • Tell approved drivers to sign in with their phone number and secure login code.
  • Run one test assignment with each new driver before sending real rider trips.

Trip Assignment Rules

  • Only assign drivers to trips with payment status PAID or Pay on Dropoff trips.
  • For prepaid trips, make sure commitment is CONFIRMED before assignment.
  • Assign one driver or courier at a time.
  • Do not reassign completed or cancelled trips.

Payment & Payout Rules

  • Prepaid trips must show PAID before dispatch.
  • Driver earnings are credited after trip completion.
  • Payout requests should be checked against the driver wallet balance.
  • After sending bank transfer to the driver, mark the payout as paid in Admin Payouts.

Rider Support & Cancellation Rules

  • If a rider has paid, confirm the trip shows PAID and CONFIRMED before assigning a driver.
  • If payment succeeds but the rider is unsure, ask them to check Trip Status using the same phone number.
  • If a rider wants to cancel before driver assignment, review the trip status before cancelling.
  • If a driver has already started the trip, do not cancel without speaking to both rider and driver.
  • If a trip is cancelled, do not assign a driver to it.
  • Keep all rider communication short, polite, and clear during launch operations.

Operations Support Contacts

  • Primary Operator: Assign one person to monitor trips, payments, drivers, and payouts during operating hours.
  • Rider Support: Use one dedicated phone number or WhatsApp line for rider payment, pickup, cancellation, and trip status questions.
  • Driver Support: Use one dedicated phone number or WhatsApp line for driver login, assigned trip, completion, wallet, and payout issues.
  • Payment Issues: Confirm payment status in Trip Detail before taking action.
  • Escalation: For payment disputes, safety issues, or serious trip problems, contact the operations operator before cancelling or reassigning the trip.

Support Notes

  • If a rider payment succeeds but status is not updated, check Trip Status again first.
  • If a driver cannot see an assigned trip, confirm the driver phone number is correct.
  • If a trip is cancelled, do not assign a driver.
  • Keep rider and driver communication clear and simple during launch operations.
